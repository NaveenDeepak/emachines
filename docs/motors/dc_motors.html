DC Motors

Executive Summary

Purpose: Analyze and design DC brushed motors

What it does: Calculate DC motor performance: back-EMF, torque, efficiency.

Why It Matters

An engineer needs to estimate torque, speed, and efficiency for a DC motor before prototyping.

Quick Start

Inputs: Motor geometry (pole pitch, slot pitch), magnetic properties (flux linkage), winding configuration (Carter coefficient)

Outputs: Back-EMF constant, torque output, efficiency curves

How It Fits Into the Motor Design Workflow

Upstream (depends on): Uses winding factors from 01_winding.ipynb,

Downstream (used by): design decisions

Carter’s Coefficient

Armature slotting increases the effective air gap by displacing flux around slot openings.

Theory: \[g_{eff} = g \times k_c\]

where: \[\gamma = \frac{(w_{so}/g)^2}{5 + w_{so}/g}, \quad k_c = \frac{\tau_s}{\tau_s - \gamma \cdot g}\]

calculate_carter_coefficient

def calculate_carter_coefficient(
    g:float, w_so:float, slot_pitch:float
)->float:

Carter’s coefficient for slot opening effect.

Accounts for the reduction in effective air gap due to slot openings. Returns a value typically between 0.8 and 1.0.

Args: g: Air gap [m] w_so: Slot opening width [m] slot_pitch: Slot pitch (distance between slots) [m]
